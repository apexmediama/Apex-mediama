Privacy policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (name, company, phone, email) and basic data about your visit.
  • We use it to contact you about your request, to run your campaigns if you hire us, and to measure our ads.
  • We do not sell, trade or transfer your personal information to outside parties.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who we are

Apex Media Leads ("Apex Media", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit apexmediama.com or use our services.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you fill out our forms, book a call or interact with our website or our ads:

  • Full name and company name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City and location information
  • Trade, service interests and preferences
  • Notes from our calls, emails and text messages with you

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card or bank account number.

3. How we use your information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Contact you regarding your inquiry or call request
  • Schedule and confirm calls and appointments
  • Provide you with information about our appointment-setting services
  • Provide our services and bill for them
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send relevant marketing communications (you may opt out at any time)

4. Calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you, including by automated means, about your request and our services. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

We do not share your mobile number or your text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Homeowners who answer our clients' ads

When a homeowner fills out a form on an ad we run for one of our contractor clients, we receive the homeowner's name, phone number, address and project details. We use that information only to call or text the homeowner about the request they made and to book an appointment with that contractor. The contractor controls that information and we handle it on their behalf.

6. Facebook Pixel and ad measurement

Our website uses the Facebook Pixel, a tracking tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc., and may use Google Ads conversion tracking. These tools help us measure the effectiveness of our advertising by understanding the actions people take on our website. They may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used to serve relevant ads to people who have visited our website.

7. Sharing your information

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties. We may share your information with trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business, such as Calendly (scheduling), Formspree (form delivery), Stripe (payments), Meta and Google (advertising), and our CRM, email and messaging providers, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

8. Data security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel.

9. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as we need it to work with you, plus what tax and legal rules require. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do it within 30 days unless the law requires us to keep it.

10. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect some functionality of our website.

11. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

12. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Opt out of text messages by replying STOP

13. Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.
