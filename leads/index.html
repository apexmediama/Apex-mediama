FAQ

Straight answers.

Where do the leads come from?

From our own Meta ads under our homeowner brand, One Roof Quote, shown only in Dallas–Fort Worth and Houston. The homeowner answers five questions, confirms their mobile number with a text code and agrees to be contacted by one roofer.

Are they really exclusive?

Yes. Each request goes to one roofer, and the homeowner's consent names one roofer. We never resell a lead, not even after you're done with it.

How fast do they arrive?

In real time, usually under a minute after the homeowner sends the form, by text, email and the LeadConnector app.

What if a lead is bad?

Report it within 72 hours with the reason. Wrong numbers, duplicates, out-of-area requests and people who didn't ask or don't own the home go back on your balance.

Can I pause or set a limit?

Yes. Set a daily cap, and text us to pause. Unused leads stay on your balance, and if you cancel we refund them.

How many leads will I get?

It depends on your area and on how many roofers share it. We'll give you an honest estimate on the call before you buy. We don't promise volumes or results.

What's the difference with the Apex appointment plan?

With leads, you call the homeowner and book the visit yourself. With the appointment plan, we run ads in your own account, call every lead and put confirmed visits on your calendar for a monthly fee.
