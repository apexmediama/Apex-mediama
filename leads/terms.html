Lead Purchase Terms

Last updated October 5, 2026

The short version

  • Every lead is exclusive: one homeowner, one buyer, never resold.
  • Leads are prepaid. Refills of 10 are charged when your balance hits 2, and we tell you first.
  • Bad numbers, duplicates, out-of-area and non-owners reported within 72 hours go back on your balance.
  • No contract. Cancel anytime and we refund your unused leads.

1. Agreement

These Lead Purchase Terms (the “Terms”) are between Apex Media Leads (“Apex,” “we”) and the business that buys leads from us (“you”). By paying for a lead package you agree to them. They work together with our website terms and privacy policy.

2. What you buy

A lead is a request from a homeowner for a roofing quote at a property inside the area you chose, collected through our own advertising under our homeowner brand, One Roof Quote. Each lead includes the homeowner’s name and mobile number, ZIP code, the type of project, timing and insurance answers, and the address when the homeowner provides it.

Every lead is exclusive: we send it to one buyer only and never resell it. The homeowner’s consent covers contact by Apex and by one (1) roofing contractor matched to the request. Phone numbers are verified by a text code or by a carrier lookup before delivery.

3. Prices and payment

  • Leads are prepaid. The trial is $825 for 5 leads, once per company. Refills are $1,650 for 10 leads.
  • When you buy, you authorize us to charge the same payment method for a 10-lead refill each time your balance drops to 2 leads. We text or email you before every refill, and you can pause refills at any time by text or email.
  • We may change prices for future packages with 14 days’ written notice. Leads already paid for keep their price.
  • Prices don’t include taxes, if any apply.

4. Delivery

We deliver leads in real time by text message, email and the LeadConnector app, inside the cities or counties you chose and up to the daily cap you set. When several buyers cover the same area, leads rotate between them. We don’t promise a number of leads per day, week or month.

5. Credits for bad leads

Report a lead within 72 hours of delivery, with the reason and how many times you tried to reach the homeowner. We add the lead back to your balance if:

  • the phone number is disconnected, wrong or not the homeowner’s;
  • it duplicates a lead we sent you in the last 30 days;
  • the property is outside the area you chose;
  • the person says they never asked for a quote, or doesn’t own the property.

We don’t credit leads because the homeowner didn’t answer, changed their mind, chose another roofer or didn’t like the price. Credits are capped at 20% of the leads delivered to you in a calendar month and are added to your balance, not paid in cash.

6. Your obligations

  • Keep a current general liability insurance certificate on file with us and a Google rating of 4.0 or higher.
  • Contact each lead promptly, ideally within 15 minutes during business hours, and give every homeowner a written quote.
  • Use each lead only to discuss the homeowner’s roofing project. Don’t resell, share or add leads to other marketing.
  • Follow the laws that apply to your own calls and texts, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, Do Not Call rules and Texas law, and honor any request to stop contact right away.
  • Protect homeowners’ personal information and delete it when they ask.

7. Pausing, cancelling and refunds

You can pause or cancel at any time by text or email. If you cancel, we refund the unused leads on your balance at the price you paid within 10 business days. Delivered leads are not refundable except as credits under section 5.

8. Removal

We may stop delivering leads to you if you don’t meet section 6, or if homeowners report misleading or unprofessional conduct. If we do, we refund your unused balance.

9. No guarantee of results

We sell leads, not appointments or jobs. We don’t guarantee that a homeowner will answer, book a visit or hire you.

10. Liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability for any claim related to these Terms is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before the claim, and neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential damages. You are responsible for, and will cover us against, claims that come from your own calls, texts, quotes or work.
