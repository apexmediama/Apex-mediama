FAQ

Straight answers.

We already tried Facebook ads. It didn't work.

Ads on their own rarely do. A lead that nobody calls within five minutes goes cold, and most contractors are on a job when the form comes in. The ads are less than half of this. The rest is calling, qualifying and booking every lead while you work.

How fast do appointments start?

Setup takes about a week: ad account, forms, call scripts and your calendar. Our target is first appointments within two to three weeks of the ads going live. The warranty clock starts the day they go live.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta, from your own ad account. The minimum is $1,500 a month. You see every dollar, you keep the data and the pixel history, and we can't hide our results inside a bundled price.

What counts as a confirmed appointment?

A homeowner in your service area who owns the property, has the project we advertised and confirmed by phone or text within 24 hours of the visit. Not a form fill and not a phone number.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. It's month to month from day one. You start with $3,000: a one-time $1,000 setup to build your system, plus your first month. After that it's $2,000 a month, billed in advance.

Cancel anytime with 7 days' written notice before your next billing date. The setup is non-refundable once we've held your kickoff call.

What do you need from me?

Admin access to your Meta ad account (we set one up if you don't have one), a calendar we can book into, $1,500 a month or more in ad spend, and someone who confirms or declines each appointment within 24 hours. Keep at least 10 hours a week open for visits.

Do you work with my trade?

We book appointments for roofing, HVAC, pools and outdoor living, foundation repair, kitchen and bath remodeling, windows and doors, and epoxy garage floors. Something else? If the job is big enough and the homeowner decides at the kitchen table, ask us on the call and we'll tell you straight whether it works.

Do you do SEO, Google Maps or websites?

Your Google Business Profile, yes. Every plan includes our Google presence package: the profile set up and kept current, automatic review requests after each job, and missed-call text-back. Websites, if you need one: a one-page site for $399, plus $119 a month for hosting, updates and edits. Full SEO, no. It takes months to pay off, and we'd rather spend your first 30 days on booked appointments.

Who calls my leads, and in what language?

Our appointment setters, fluent in English and Spanish. They call every new lead within five minutes during business hours, in whichever language the homeowner prefers, qualify them and book the visit on your calendar. You can see every call and every note.
