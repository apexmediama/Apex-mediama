Refund policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version

  • $3,000 to start: a one-time $1,000 setup plus your first month.
  • Then $2,000 a month, billed in advance. Month to month.
  • Cancel with 7 days' written notice before your next billing date.
  • The setup is non-refundable once your kickoff call has taken place.
  • The warranty gives you a free month of management, not a cash refund.

1. Before your kickoff call

If you cancel in writing before your kickoff call, we refund your start payment in full, minus any card or bank processing fees our payment processor doesn't return to us.

2. The setup fee

The $1,000 setup covers building your campaigns, lead forms, call scripts, calendar and reminders. It's non-refundable once your kickoff call has taken place.

3. Monthly management

Management is billed in advance, once a month. You can cancel at any time with 7 days' written notice before your next billing date. Your service runs until the end of the month you've paid for. We don't give partial-month refunds or prorate a month once it has started.

4. The warranty

If we don't put at least five confirmed appointments on your calendar (three for pools and kitchen and bath remodeling) within 30 days of your ads going live, your next month of management is free. It applies when your ad spend stays at $1,500 a month or more and the ads stay live, you confirm or decline each appointment within 24 hours, and you keep at least 10 hours a week open for visits.

The remedy is a free month of management, not a cash refund. Tell us in writing, we'll review the calendar together, and the free month is applied to your next billing date.

5. If we don't deliver

If we fail to provide the services you paid for, for example if your campaigns go unmanaged for 14 or more consecutive days without notice, you can ask for a prorated refund for that period.

6. Ad spend

You pay Meta directly for your ad spend, from your own ad account. Refunds or credits for ad spend are governed by Meta's policies.

7. Disputed charges

Please talk to us before disputing a charge with your bank. Most issues get solved in one call. If a charge is disputed without contacting us first, we'll pause services while the dispute is open and give the bank a copy of your signed agreement and the records of the work.

8. How to ask for a refund

Email us with your company name, the invoice or receipt number, and the reason. We reply within 2 business days and resolve requests within 10 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method: 5 to 10 business days for cards and 3 to 7 business days for bank (ACH) payments.

9. Changes

Changes to this policy apply to new payments. A signed agreement keeps the policy that was in effect when it was signed.
