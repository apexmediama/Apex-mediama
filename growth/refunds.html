Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Setup fee

The $497 setup fee is refunded in full if you cancel before onboarding starts, meaning before you give us access to your accounts or we begin building your system. Once onboarding starts, the setup fee is not refundable.

Monthly fees

The $997 monthly fee covers the month ahead and is not refunded for partial months. During the first 3 months the full term applies; after that you can cancel with 30 days' notice and you won't be charged again.

Work guarantee

If in any month we don't deliver the 12 posts or the 4 new ads, your next monthly fee is reduced by 50%. This credit is applied automatically on your next invoice.

Ad spend

Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta and other platforms, so we can't refund it. You can lower or pause it at any time.

How to cancel or ask for a refund

Write to apexmediama@gmail.com from the email on your account. We confirm every request within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
