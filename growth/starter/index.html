Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I keep my business number?

Yes. You answer your phone like always. When you don't pick up, the call goes to Ava. We send you the 2-minute steps for your carrier (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile or your office phone).

What does Ava say on a call?

She greets callers with your business name, answers basic questions from the information you give us, takes their name, number and what they need, and tells them you'll call back. She never makes up prices or promises.

Which plan is right for me?

If you mainly miss calls, start with Ava Starter. If you want Ava to book appointments and answer your website chat, choose the $397 plan. If you want more customers coming in through ads, content and Google, that's the Growth Plan: book a 15-minute call and we'll tell you honestly if it fits.

What if I use more than 150 minutes?

150 minutes is about 75 calls. If you get close, we'll tell you and talk about options. We never charge anything extra without your OK.

Will callers know Ava is an AI?

If someone asks, she says she's an AI assistant for your business. She speaks naturally in English and Spanish and switches when the caller does.

Can I get the summary by text message?

From day one you get it by email. Text summaries can be added once the carriers approve your business number, which takes 1 to 3 weeks.

How do I cancel?

Ava Starter is month to month. Email apexmediama@gmail.com before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. Monthly fees aren't refunded for partial months.
