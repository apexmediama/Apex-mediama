Questions

Straight answers

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can promise a number of customers. We guarantee the work: 12 posts, 4 new ads and your follow-up system running every month. If we miss it, your next month is half price.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta with your own card, so you see every dollar. We recommend starting at $450 a month.

Why a 3-month minimum?

Ads need 30 to 60 days to learn and settle. After month 3 it's month to month and you can leave with 30 days' notice.

Do I own my accounts and content?

Yes. Your ad account, pages, profiles and the content you paid for are yours. We work through partner access.

Do you need my passwords?

Never. You add us as a partner in Meta Business Manager and as a manager on your Google profile.

What do I need to give you?

Photos or short videos of your work, 30 minutes for a kickoff call, and approvals within 48 hours.

How fast do we launch?

7 days from signing. Text messaging can take 1 to 3 weeks while carriers approve your number; everything else starts on day 7.

Do you work with my kind of business?

If you're a local business doing $25,000 or more a month, probably yes. Book a call and we'll tell you honestly.
